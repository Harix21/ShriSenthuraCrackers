Important Safety Guidelines

Simple precautions can make your celebration safer and more enjoyable. Always read and follow the instructions and warnings supplied with each product.

01

Wear Safe Clothing

Wear well-fitting cotton clothes and closed footwear. Keep loose clothing, hair and accessories away from flames and sparks.

02

Choose an Open Area

Use fireworks only in an appropriate open space, away from buildings, vehicles, dry vegetation and other flammable materials.

03

Adult Supervision

Children should only participate under responsible adult supervision and according to the product's safety instructions.

04

Keep Water Nearby

Keep water, sand or suitable fire-safety equipment nearby so that you can respond quickly if something goes wrong.

05

Never Relight

If a firework fails to ignite, do not immediately approach or relight it. Wait and follow the instructions supplied with the product.

06

Keep Your Distance

Light one item at a time and move to a safe distance. Keep your face and hands away from the ignition point.

07

Buy Legal Products

Purchase fireworks from trusted sellers and use only products that are permitted under applicable local rules.

08

Clean Up Safely

Let used fireworks cool completely before handling waste. Dispose of spent materials responsibly and keep the celebration area clean.

✓

Do's

Read all product labels, instructions and warnings before use.
Use fireworks in a clear and suitable outdoor area.
Keep people, animals, vehicles and property at a safe distance.
Keep water or sand readily available.
Supervise children closely.
Buy from authorized and trusted sellers.
×

Don'ts

Don't use fireworks indoors or close to flammable materials.
Don't hold active fireworks in your hands.
Don't aim fireworks at people, animals, vehicles or buildings.
Don't use damaged, altered or unauthorized products.
Don't relight a failed firework.
Don't leave burning or ignited fireworks unattended.
Important

Celebrate Smart. Celebrate Safe.

If an accident or fire occurs, move away from danger, keep others at a safe distance and contact the relevant local emergency service when necessary. For injuries or burns, seek appropriate first aid and professional medical care.

Fire: Move away from danger and use appropriate fire-safety equipment only when it is safe to do so.
Injury: Stop the activity and seek suitable first aid or medical attention.
Emergency: Contact the relevant local emergency service for immediate danger.
